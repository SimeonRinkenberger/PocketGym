What PocketGym is

You save a link to a fitness video. PocketGym reads what is publicly attached to that video — the caption, the description, and where those say nothing, the audio or the words on screen — and turns it into a workout card you can follow and log. It also gives you a weekly plan, a progress view, and a coach called Pumpy who can build workouts out of what you have saved.

PocketGym links to source videos and shows the platforms' own players. Files you choose to upload and source material used for supported AI reads may pass through temporary storage, as described in the privacy policy. PocketGym also includes an exercise bank, workout edits and swaps, reminders, and optional Apple Watch, widget and Live Activity features on supported Apple devices.
